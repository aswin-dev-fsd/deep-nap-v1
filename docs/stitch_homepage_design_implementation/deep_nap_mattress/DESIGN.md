## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Ethos
This design system represents a bespoke mattress manufacturer rooted in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. The brand identity balances artisanal integrity with modern industrial precision: comfortable, dependable, transparent, and honest. It rejects artificial e-commerce urgency (no flash sales, countdown timers, or cart drawers) in favor of consultative, enquiry-first relationships.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for discerning homeowners, hospitality purveyors, and regional buyers seeking bespoke sleep solutions tailored to regional climates. The interface instills calm assurance, tactile credibility, and structural transparency through authentic workshop photography, measured typographic cadence, and zero visual clutter.

### Design Movement & Visual Style
The visual execution embodies **Warm Minimalist Tactility**:
- Crisp hairline infrastructure (1px precision borders).
- Warm organic surfaces anchored by deep maritime foundations.
- Restrained luxury where gold is treated as an intentional instrument, never an expanse.
- Utilitarian typography paired with welcoming geometric display forms.

## Colors

### Core Color Roles
- **Primary (`#142C47`)**: Structural foundation used for primary utility anchors, headers, footers, high-emphasis action triggers, active radio/checkbox states, and track selectors.
- **Secondary (`#EFE5D7`)**: The tactile parchment base. Serves as inverted text on navy surfaces, modal backdrops, sticky enquiry containers, and contextual card fills.
- **Tertiary (`#DCA544`)**: Active focus indicator. Strictly bound to less than 5% of any visible viewport. Reserved for star ratings, active firmness top notches, key dimensional figures, and micro status points. Never applied as wide surface washes or linear gradients.
- **Neutral (`#F8F4ED`)**: The ambient canvas base that softens digital glare and simulates unbleached organic cotton.

### Named Colors & Constraints
- **Navy Deep (`#0E1F33`)**: Grounding foundation for persistent global footers and specification tables.
- **Gold Soft (`#F3E3C2`)**: Warm informational tint used for custom size callout bands, quote notification panels, and advisory badges.
- **Ink (`#1A2230`)**: High-contrast body typography ensuring legible long-form reading on light canvases.
- **Slate (`#5E6B7A`)**: Secondary descriptive copy, form field titles, dimensional qualifiers, and breadcrumb trails.
- **Hairline (`#DFD8CC`)**: 1px structural definition across all surfaces.
- **Prohibited**: Pink hues of any saturation or lightness are strictly banned across visual assets, illustrations, and UI tokens.

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy & Styling
- **Sentence Case Discipline**: All display, headline, and title elements must follow sentence case formatting. Full uppercase is strictly restricted to `overline` labels (e.g., category markers) and specific legal acronyms.
- **Display Presence**: Headlines leverage the balanced geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans with controlled negative letter spacing to preserve an editorial rhythm without geometric harshness.
- **Body & Functional Clarity**: Inter handles all contextual reading, transactional tables, parameter inputs, and price metrics.
- **Multilingual Tamil Pairing**: When paired with regional Tamil copy (Noto Sans Tamil or Mukta Malar), set the Tamil text one typographic scale tier lower than the companion English label to equalize baseline optical density.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Core Unit**: Strict 8px baseline rhythm across all horizontal paddings, component heights, and vertical module flows.
- **Desktop System**: 12-column responsive fluid grid within a 1280px maximum content envelope. Section dividers sit horizontally on the 8px baseline grid.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop`: $\ge$ 1024px (12 columns, 32px gutters, 48px canvas margin).
  - `Tablet`: 640px – 1023px (8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px canvas margin).
  - `Mobile`: $\le$ 639px (4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px canvas margin).
- **Contained Columns**:
  - Custom Size Builder: Centered at 820px.
  - Editorial & Content Policy Panels: Centered at 720px.
  - Warranty Claim Module: Centered at 640px.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tactile Flat Architecture
Visual stratification is achieved via structural hairline boundaries, layered parchment tints, and selective card borders rather than diffuse drop shadows.

- **Hairline Framing**: Standard surface elements exist at elevation zero, demarcated strictly by `1px solid #DFD8CC`.
- **The Single-Shadow Policy**: Only one surface element per screen may deploy a directional blur: the sticky mobile bottom enquiry tray (`0px -4px 20px rgba(20, 44, 71, 0.08)`).
- **Physical Elevation via Inset & Edge Rules**: Featured mattresses and recommended firmness nodes establish focus through a top border accent (`4px solid #DCA544`) paired with an interior background shift to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Focus Rings**: Form fields and interactive elements indicate focus using a sharp `2px solid #142C47` outline positioned with an offset of 2px.

## Shapes

### Form Geometry & Radii
- **Level 2 Structural Radius**: Default cards, actionable inputs, and dialogue modules deploy an `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Large Panels & Modals (`rounded-lg`)**: Primary configuration wrappers, quotation builder shells, and photographic focal cards scale to `12px` (`0.75rem`) or `16px` (`1rem`).
- **Interactive Targets**: Primary inquiry buttons leverage an exact `8px` corner radius.
- **Functional Exceptions (Pill `9999px`)**: Pill geometry is strictly reserved for:
  - Filter and dimension chips.
  - Header contact triggers and floating WhatsApp connectors.
  - Status indicators and firmware rating nodes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Enquiry Button**: Minimum height of 48px on desktop, 52px on mobile. Solid `#142C47` fill with `#EFE5D7` text, 8px border radius, and Inter Medium 15px.
- **Secondary / Outline Button**: 1.5px solid `#142C47` border, transparent surface, `#142C47` typography.
- **WhatsApp Integration Button**: Pill shape (`9999px`), `#142C47` or `#0E1F33` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, paired with a small 6px `#DCA544` dot preceding the copy.

### Cards
- **Product & Tier Cards**: Built on `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F4ED` surfaces encased in a 1px `#DFD8CC` hairline border with a 12px border radius. Top edge features an optional 4px accent indicator (`#DCA544` on highlighted models).
- **Interactive Question Cards (Mattress Quiz)**: Minimum vertical touch boundary of 120px, framed by 1px `#DFD8CC`. Selection changes border to 2px solid `#142C47`.

### Chips & Selectors
- **Attribute Chips**: 32px height, pill geometry (`9999px`), 1px `#DFD8CC` border, `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Active Filter State**: Background flips to `#142C47` with inverted `#EFE5D7` label.

### Form Inputs
- **Text & Dimensional Fields**: 52px fixed height, 8px radius, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#DFD8CC` default border. Focused state replaces default border with a 2px `#142C47` ring. Validation errors deploy a 1px `#C84B31` perimeter with an accompanying 13px warning label.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px structural squares/circles with a 1.5px `#142C47` border. Active states fill with `#142C47` containing a clean tick or inner dot.

### Specialized System Elements
- **Interactive Firmness Scale**: A horizontal segmented track rendered in `#DFD8CC` with live values populated in `#142C47`. The active dial uses an 18px circular thumb flanked by small `#DCA544` callout markers.
- **Mobile Sticky Enquiry Bar**: Persistent 72px base bar at screen bottom, `#EFE5D7` background with a 1px `#DFD8CC` top border and ambient anchor shadow. Houses direct phone enquiry and WhatsApp dialogue triggers.
